## Design Specificity Verdict

**Highly domain-specific.** Procedure categories (Consulta, Limpieza, Empaste, Extracción, etc.), appointment status workflow, photo comparison with before/after, and follow-up notifications (30+ days without appointment) are all specific to dental/medical practice management. This is not a generic CRUD app with a medical skin.

---

## Overall Impression

The app has moved from "functional but rough" to "genuinely polished medical tool." The critical safety and consistency issues have been resolved. The remaining gaps are in advanced UX polish (undo, auto-save, contextual help) rather than fundamental usability problems.

---

## What's Working

1. **Domain authenticity**: Procedure categories, appointment status workflow, photo comparison, follow-up notifications — all reflect real medical practice needs
2. **Component consistency**: Every screen uses the same Card/ConfirmDialog/EmptyState/FAB/Header pattern — zero deviation
3. **Safety-critical fixes**: Double-booking prevention and ConfirmDialog standardization are exactly the right priorities for a medical tool

---

## Priority Issues

### P2 — Minor

1. **WelcomeOverlay `onComplete` is a no-op** — Either remove the prop or wire it up
2. **Artificial 300ms loading delays** — Cosmetic, adds unnecessary latency if data loads instantly

### P3 — Polish

3. **No undo for destructive actions** — ConfirmDialog says "no se puede deshacer"
4. **No auto-save or draft persistence for forms** — Work lost on accidental navigation
5. **NotificationsScreen has no loading skeleton** — Breaks the pattern
6. **PhotoCompareScreen slider lacks grab cursor** — No visual indicator it's draggable

---

## Remaining Questions

1. Should there be a help section or FAQ for new users?
2. Should forms auto-save drafts to localStorage?
3. Should the undo mechanism be added to the toast system?
