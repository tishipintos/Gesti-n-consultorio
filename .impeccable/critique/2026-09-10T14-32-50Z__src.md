## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Partially grounded. The notification system (auto-generated follow-up reminders after 30+ days), photo comparison with three clinical modes, and appointment density dots on the calendar are genuinely medical-office-specific. However, the overall visual language (glass material, teal palette, DM Sans) is category-interchangeable — could be a food delivery or fitness app. No medical-specific color coding, procedure icons, or patient photo identification.

**Deterministic scan:** 3 findings — all `bounce-easing` warnings on `--ease-spring` in `index.css:34,157,165`. The overshoot (1.56) is used for modal entrances and FAB only — contextually appropriate, not a slop issue. No false positives from the detector.

---

## Overall Impression

A solid, well-crafted MVP with production-grade foundations. The fluid responsive system, consistent design tokens, and Argentine Spanish localization are genuinely good. The biggest missed opportunity is domain specificity — this could be any app's UI skin applied to medical data. The notification intelligence (auto follow-up) is the standout feature that proves the product understands its users.

---

## What's Working

1. **Argentine Spanish localization is excellent** — "¿Estás seguro de que querés eliminar este turno?" feels native, not translated. Locale-aware date formatting via date-fns.

2. **Photo comparison with three modes** (single, side-by-side, slider) is a genuinely useful clinical feature — most medical apps don't offer this. The slider with keyboard support is well-implemented.

3. **Notification system is intelligent** — auto-generates follow-up reminders when a patient hasn't visited in 30+ days. This is a real clinical need, not just "upcoming appointments."

## Priority Issues

### P0 — Blocking

1. **`alert()` used for validation errors in PhotoFormScreen**
   - `src/screens/PhotoFormScreen.tsx:41,58` — uses `alert('La imagen no puede superar 5MB')` and `alert('Seleccioná una imagen')`
   - Breaks the polished UI, inconsistent with the toast system, jarring on mobile
   - **Fix:** Replace with toast calls or inline error messages
   - **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

2. **No double-booking prevention**
   - `AppointmentFormScreen.tsx` doesn't check if the selected date/time slot is already occupied
   - In a real office, this causes scheduling conflicts
   - **Fix:** Validate against existing appointments before saving
   - **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

### P1 — Major

3. **Inconsistent delete patterns**
   - Calendar/Client detail: uses `ConfirmDialog` modal
   - Photo compare: uses inline two-button confirmation
   - Photo delete on client detail: uses a tiny `×` button with no confirmation
   - **Fix:** Standardize on ConfirmDialog for all destructive actions
   - **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

4. **No undo mechanism for destructive actions**
   - Deleting a patient, appointment, or photo is immediate and irreversible
   - **Fix:** Add a brief undo window (3-5 seconds) via toast with action button
   - **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

5. **"Turno" vs "cita" terminology inconsistency**
   - CalendarScreen: "Turnos", "Agendar turno"
   - AppointmentFormScreen: "Nueva cita", "Agendar cita"
   - **Fix:** Standardize on "turno" (more common in Argentine dental offices)
   - **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### P2 — Minor

6. **No onboarding or first-run experience**
   - New users land on an empty calendar with no guidance
   - **Fix:** Add a brief onboarding overlay or first-use tooltips
   - **Command:** `/impeccable onboard`

7. **Photo compare `selectedPhoto` index not bounds-checked**
   - `src/screens/PhotoCompareScreen.tsx:134` — if photos are deleted while viewing, index can become stale
   - **Fix:** Clamp `selectedPhoto` to valid range
   - **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

8. **Warning color `#D4A574` may not pass WCAG AA**
   - On white backgrounds, too low-contrast for follow-up notification badges
   - **Fix:** Darken to at least `#B8895A`
   - **Command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### P3 — Polish

9. **Empty state on compare screen uses Card instead of EmptyState component**
   - **Fix:** Use EmptyState for consistency
   - **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

10. **`setTimeout(() => navigate(-1), 300)` in AppointmentFormScreen**
    - Arbitrary delay before navigation, may feel sluggish
    - **Fix:** Navigate immediately after toast
    - **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Questions to Consider

1. **Should the calendar be the home screen, or should it be a dashboard showing today's summary?** A dashboard with "today's patients," "upcoming," and "needs follow-up" might be more useful than a raw calendar view.

2. **Why does creating an appointment require navigating to the client list first?** The FAB on calendar says "Agendar" but routes to `/clients`. Should there be a direct "New Appointment" flow from the calendar?

3. **Should patient photos be the primary identification method?** The data model has no patient photo field — avatars are generated from initials. In a real office, visual identification matters.

4. **What happens when the office has 500+ patients?** The client list has no pagination or virtualization. The calendar re-renders all appointments on every date selection.

5. **Should there be a treatment plan view?** The app tracks individual appointments but doesn't group them into treatment plans. For dental/medical offices, treatment plans are the core workflow.
